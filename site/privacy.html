Privacy

Last updated 2026-10-28.

The app

Acreage reads file names, sizes and dates on your computer to draw its map. That information stays on your computer. The app has no account, sends no telemetry, has no crash reporter that calls home, and makes no network requests of its own.

It writes two kinds of files, both under your home folder: settings in ~/.config/acreage/config.toml, and reports only where you choose to save them. Files you move to Trash go to your system's Trash, where you can restore them.
